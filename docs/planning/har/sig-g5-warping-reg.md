### Recommended Derivation Formula for doc3d 3D Mesh

The recommended approach uses normalized standard deviation of the Z (depth) coordinates across the document mesh grid:

```text
warping_severity = clip(k * std(Z_grid_normalized), 0.0, 1.0)
```

Where:

- `Z_grid_normalized`: depth values of mesh vertices, normalized to [0,1] range within each document
- `std(Z_grid_normalized)`: standard deviation across all mesh vertex depths — captures overall deviation from flatness
- `k`: empirical calibration constant tuned so that a visually "moderate" warp (e.g., page_curl visible but text readable) maps to ~0.4–0.5, and extreme distortion maps to ~0.9+

**Rationale**: std(Z) is simple to compute, interpretable (measures spread from mean depth = flatness), robust to local outliers, and avoids complex surface normal calculations. It naturally produces 0.0 for a perfectly flat mesh. The constant `k` must be calibrated by visual inspection of ~50 doc3d samples at various computed severity levels before committing to labels.

**Alternative formula** (secondary validation): `severity = clip(max_displacement / document_diagonal, 0, 1)` — captures worst-case deformation relative to document size. Both formulas should be computed during the calibration phase; if they disagree by >0.2 for the same sample, that sample should be flagged for human review.

### Label Convention

`warping_severity = 0.0` means FLAT document (no warping present). `warping_severity = 1.0` means extreme curl or perspective distortion where text lines are severely non-linear. Intermediate values: ~0.15 = light curl visible at page edge, ~0.3 = moderate page curl (text distorted at margins), ~0.5 = significant perspective or curl (multi-line curvature visible), ~0.7 = strong deformation (text at page center is distorted). This convention must NEVER use 0.0 to mean "unable to measure" — a separate `warping_measurement_failed` boolean flag must be used for that case. The derivation formula must map naturally to this scale and must be validated against human perception on 50+ calibration images before committing to label extraction.

**Q1 — Is the doc3d 3D mesh approach correct, and what formula?**

Confirmed correct strategy. Using the 3D mesh geometry is the right approach as it avoids subjective human annotation and provides precise geometric ground truth — the gold standard for a regression head. Recommended formula: `severity = clip(k * std(Z_grid), 0, 1)` where k is empirically tuned. The std of the normalized depth coordinates captures overall deviation from flatness without complex surface normal calculations. The calibration constant k must be tuned by reviewing sample renderings to align computed values with visual "mild," "moderate," and "severe" categories. This aligns with the analyst's independent recommendation in Section 2.

**Q2 — Are the 4 P0 blockers correctly identified and prioritized?**

Confirmed. All four P0 blockers represent a complete halt to progress. Blockers 1, 2, and 4 (labeling script, formula, L2 field) are strictly sequential dependencies: without the formula, the script cannot be written; without the script, the L2 field cannot be populated. **Q3 — Does warping type diversity (perspective/page_curl/fold) adequately cover real-world patterns?**

Insufficient. The three types cover camera-capture and geometric distortion scenarios but miss common physical degradation types. The OOD set (ADF curl) correctly identifies one real-world pattern gap, but it is an error to leave ADF curl out of training entirely. Production document scanners frequently use ADF mechanisms; a model that only encounters ADF curl at OOD evaluation time has a systematic training-to-deployment mismatch.

**Q4 — Most critical missing warping pattern beyond ADF curl?**

Creasing/crumpling (wet/humidity-induced cockling). This involves high-frequency, sharp geometric changes not present in the smooth page_curl, fold, or perspective types. Crumpled paper creates a fundamentally different visual texture from smooth deformations — crinkle patterns have spatial frequency an order of magnitude higher than page curl. A model trained only on smooth warp types will assign near-zero severity to heavily cockled paper, causing systematic failure on aged, water-damaged, or heavily-handled documents.

**Q5 — Overall rating:**

BLOCKED. The project is non-viable in its current state. The absence of a defined labeling formula and a source for 40% of the training data (NONE class) means the dataset cannot be assembled.
