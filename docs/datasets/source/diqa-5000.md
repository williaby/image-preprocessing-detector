##### 2.5 Annotation Schema Details

> **Format**: CSV with MOS scores linking enhanced images to originals

**CSV Schema** (train.csv, val.csv, test.csv):

```csv
res,ori,overall,sharpness,color_fidelity
enhanced_001.jpg,original_001.jpg,3.8,4.1,3.5
enhanced_002.jpg,original_001.jpg,4.2,4.5,3.9
```

**Key Fields for Parsing**:

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
|-------|------|----------|-------|
| `res` | str | Yes | Enhanced image filename (what we process) |
| `ori` | str | Yes | Original distorted image reference |
| `overall` | float | Yes | Overall quality MOS (1-5 scale, higher = better) |
| `sharpness` | float | Yes | Sharpness MOS (1-5 scale) |
| `color_fidelity` | float | Yes | Color fidelity MOS (1-5 scale) |

**MOS Scale**: 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent), collected from 15 subjects per image

| Check | Result | Notes |

| MOS Sharpness Correlation | Spearman r=0.18 | Expected: within-group variance explains low r (see notes) |
| Monotonic Bucket Trend | Kruskal-Wallis H=141.6 (p=1.7e-30) | MOS increases monotonically across RQ buckets |

#### 7. Limitations

- **Enhancement bias**: All `res/` images are algorithmically enhanced versions of `ori/` originals, not independently captured
- **Language skew**: 76% Chinese, 22% English - limited script diversity
- **Domain concentration**: 92% academic/scientific content (EDU+SCI+TEC)
- **MOS only for res/**: The 500 `ori/` base images lack MOS annotations (no quality ground truth for originals)
- **No text ground truth**: No OCR transcription provided in source - OCR from Docling GPU extraction only
- **Rotation**: 22% of images are rotated 90 degrees CW, affecting OCR and downstream processing
- **has_figure accuracy**: Layout-derived has_figure flag has 61% agreement with visual inspection (over-detection)
- **Benchmark only**: This dataset should NEVER be used for training - reserved for IQA benchmark evaluation

### 13.3 Corpus Role & Constraints

DIQA-5000 is the project's gold-standard IQA benchmark, holding 3-dimensional human MOS scores (overall, sharpness, color_fidelity) from 15 annotators per image alongside 8 classical IQA detector labels and 5,499 character-height-aware resolution quality scores. Despite this rich label density — which would nominally qualify it as ✅ Primary for IQA heads G1-1 through G1-6 and MNV4-H3 — the dataset carries a firm benchmark-only designation ("NEVER train on this dataset") to preserve the integrity of model evaluation. All 22 training heads therefore receive ❌ for DIQA-5000, and dataloaders must explicitly exclude it from training splits; its correct role is held-out calibration and SRCC/PLCC correlation testing against model predictions on the SIG-G1 and MNV4-H3 heads.
